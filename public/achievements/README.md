# Achievement Images

Place your achievement / hackathon / NCC certificate or event photos in this folder:

1. **`ncc.jpg`** (or `.png` / `.webp`) — Under Officer, National Cadet Corps (NCC) photo/certificate
2. **`code-nakshatra.jpg`** (or `.png` / `.webp`) — Code-Nakshatra II National Hackathon photo/certificate
3. **`hackcrux.jpg`** (or `.png` / `.webp`) — HackCrux LNMIIT Jaipur Hackathon photo/certificate

You can also update the filenames in `src/data/portfolio.ts` under `achievements`.
